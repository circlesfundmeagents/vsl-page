About CirclesFundMe

A Different Path to Owning the Vehicle You Drive

CirclesFundMe is an app that helps you save money, build good credit, and get financing for a vehicle. We help drivers move from renting a vehicle to owning one.

Our main focus is Mobility Finance. We help drivers save for and finance the tricycles, buses, and vehicles they use to earn money. Pick a weekly or monthly savings plan that fits your income. Build a strong savings record. Then unlock a payment plan to get the vehicle you need.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Is the program suitable for women? +

Yes! CirclesFundMe is open to any driver ready to save regularly. It doesn't matter if you're a man or a woman.

How long before I qualify for financing? +

It depends on how much and how often you save. Many members qualify after just a few months of steady saving.

Do I need to be part of a trust group to join? +

No. You can join alone or with a group. Joining with a group can help you qualify for a bigger loan.

What vehicles can I finance? +

Tricycles, buses, cars, and big trucks — as long as you use them to earn money.

I don't have collateral. Is that okay? +

No problem. Instead of collateral, we look at your savings record, your income, and your group's support.
